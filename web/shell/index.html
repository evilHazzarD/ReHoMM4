    Languages and other builds
    
Game languages. GOG's English, German, French, Italian and Spanish installers work as
      they are; Polish, Russian and Czech ones need GOG's English heroes4.exe picked with
      them. A game installed in another language than English is played in it.

    
Translations (of the English game, chosen in the game's settings, ⚙): none
      available here now, from
      ReHoMM4-translations.

    
This page plays in a desktop browser: current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

    
As an app, in its own full-screen window: Chrome or Edge's Install (in the address
      bar or the menu), Safari's Add to Dock, or on an iPad Share → Add to Home Screen.
      The app keeps its own game files (on an iPad the installer is picked again in the app).

    
Apps (on the releases page): Mac (macOS 13+, Apple chip or Intel), iPhone and iPad (iOS 17+),
      Android phones, tablets and TVs (64-bit, Android 10+), Windows PC (64-bit, 10 or 11), Linux
      PC (x86-64, 64-bit ARM) and Steam Deck, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 (homebrew).
